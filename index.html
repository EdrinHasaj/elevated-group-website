About Elevated Coaching Group

Helping Leaders Grow So Businesses Can Too.

Founded in 2019, Elevated Coaching Group is a boutique leadership development, executive coaching, and strategic HR advisory firm.

We partner with organizations at pivotal moments of growth — developing stronger leaders, more capable managers, and high-performing teams equipped to navigate change, complexity, and rapid business growth.

Our work combines executive coaching, emotional intelligence, leadership development, and strategic HR expertise to help organizations scale their people alongside their business.

Whether coaching one executive or partnering with an entire leadership team, our purpose remains the same:

Helping people unlock their potential so businesses can reach theirs.

Leadership — About Joana

Meet Joana Oliveira

Joana Oliveira is the Founder of Elevated Coaching Group and a senior Human Resources executive with more than 15 years of experience helping organizations build exceptional leaders, scale high-performing teams, and navigate transformational growth.

Throughout her career, Joana has partnered with founders, CEOs, executive teams, and boards across technology, SaaS, financial services, healthcare, consumer, and not-for-profit organizations. She has helped organizations evolve from startup to scale-up environments, supporting periods of rapid growth, organizational transformation, executive transitions, and leadership development.

Read Joana's full background

Having spent her career inside high-growth organizations, Joana understands firsthand that as businesses grow, leadership becomes the competitive advantage.

Her coaching blends executive experience with behavioural science, psychology, neuroscience, emotional intelligence, and strengths-based coaching to help leaders gain clarity, build confidence, improve relationships, and lead with greater impact.

Clients describe her as approachable, authentic, insightful, and highly practical. She creates a trusted space where leaders are challenged to think differently, discover their strengths, and unlock what she calls their “superpowers.”

A lifelong learner with an endless curiosity about people and leadership, Joana is passionate about helping leaders become the best version of themselves while building organizations where people and performance thrive together.

What Makes Us Different

Coaching Backed by Data.

Most executive coaches rely on conversation alone. At Elevated Coaching Group, coaching starts with insight.

Emotional Intelligence (EQ-i 2.0®) is the foundation of our methodology — giving leaders measurable, evidence-based insight into how they lead, communicate, influence, decide, and build relationships. Unlike IQ, emotional intelligence isn't fixed. It can be intentionally developed through coaching, practice, and feedback.

Assessment 01

EQ-i 2.0 Individual Assessment

Measures 15 emotional intelligence competencies to identify leadership strengths and development opportunities.

Assessment 02

EQ 360 Assessment

Compares self-perception with confidential feedback from managers, peers, and direct reports to uncover blind spots and accelerate growth.

Assessment 03

Team Emotional Intelligence

Reveals a leadership team's collective strengths and gaps to improve trust, collaboration, and effectiveness.

Included

Personalized Debrief, Development Plan & Coaching Integration

Every assessment includes a personalized debrief, development plan, and coaching integration.
