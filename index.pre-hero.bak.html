About Elevated Coaching Group

Leadership Development Grounded in Real Operating Experience.

Founded in 2019 by Joana Oliveira, Elevated Coaching Group is a boutique executive coaching, leadership development, and strategic HR advisory firm.

Joana brings more than 15 years of senior HR experience inside high-growth organizations. She partners with founders, executives, and leadership teams to navigate growth, change, and the people challenges that come with both.

Joana's approach and credentials

Practical, evidence-led coaching grounded in executive experience, behavioural science, emotional intelligence, and strengths-based practice.

  • Certified Human Resources Leader (CHRL)
  • Certified Executive Coach
  • Professional Certified Coach (PCC)
  • Certified EQ-i 2.0 & EQ 360 Practitioner
  • Certified Situational Leadership® (SLII) Facilitator
  • Graduated from McMaster University; training from Sheridan College & Royal Roads University

Team Effectiveness

Five Workshops for Stronger Leadership Teams.

Based on Patrick Lencioni's The Five Dysfunctions of a Team model, each workshop addresses one barrier to team effectiveness.

Series Outcome A high-trust team ready to lead through growth and change.
Foundation: Trust Collective Results
  1. 01
    Barrier Absence of Trust
    Workshop

    Building Trust

    Vulnerability-based exercises help the team speak candidly, admit mistakes, and ask for help.

  2. 02
    Barrier Fear of Conflict
    Workshop

    Mastering Conflict

    Normalize productive, unfiltered debate around ideas without damaging relationships.

  3. 03
    Barrier Lack of Commitment
    Workshop

    Achieving Commitment

    Build clarity and buy-in so the team commits fully to decisions, even amid disagreement.

  4. 04
    Barrier Avoidance of Accountability
    Workshop

    Embracing Accountability

    Build peer-to-peer accountability so the team holds itself to shared standards.

  5. 05
    Barrier Inattention to Results
    Workshop

    Focusing on Results

    Align the team around collective outcomes over individual agendas or status.
